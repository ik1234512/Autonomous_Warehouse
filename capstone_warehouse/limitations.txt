The project currently runs in a simulated warehouse environment, so its behaviour may not fully reflect real-world conditions. 
The system also depends on the accuracy of its sensor estimates and the decisions made by its planning algorithms. 
While the LLM handles user commands, all actions must pass through validation and safety checks before execution.\
These checks help prevent invalid assignments, collisions and unsafe movements.
However, they do not guarantee complete safety, so the system should still be tested thoroughly before any real-world deployment.
